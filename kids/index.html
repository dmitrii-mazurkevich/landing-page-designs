Questions

Before you book

From what age?

From 4 years. Younger kids come to the family class with a parent.

What if we miss a class?

You make it up in another group within the same month.

Do we need special clothes?

No, we give aprons. Still, clothes you do not mind is a good idea.

Can parents stay?

In the trial class, yes. Later kids usually do better on their own.
