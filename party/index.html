Questions

Before you book

How many kids can come?

Up to 25 kids with 1 host. For a bigger party we add a second host.

How early should I book?

Weekends 2-3 weeks ahead, weekdays 5 days ahead.

Can the party be at our home?

Yes. We need about 12 square metres free for the games.

What if my child gets ill?

We move the date for free if you tell us 24 hours before.
