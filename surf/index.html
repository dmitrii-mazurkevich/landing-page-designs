Questions

Before you book

I cannot swim well. Can I surf?

Yes. Beginners stay waist-deep, and the soft board floats like a raft.

Do I need a wetsuit?

No, it is included, as well as the board.

From what age?

Kids from 8 years in the kids group, adults with no upper limit.

What if there are no waves?

We move the lesson for free or run a balance and paddling session.
